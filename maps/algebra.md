# Algebra — structural map

This is a navigational map, not a rigid classification.

## Objects and maps

- [Modules](../concepts/modules.md) generalize vector spaces by replacing a field with a ring.
- [Exact sequences](../concepts/exact-sequences.md) encode kernels, images, quotients and extension problems.
- [Group extensions](../concepts/group-extensions.md) ask how a normal subgroup and its quotient assemble into a whole group.
- [Representations](../concepts/representations.md) convert abstract group actions into linear operators.
- [Field extensions](../concepts/field-extensions.md) let multiplication by algebraic elements act as linear maps.

## Geometric and computational realizations

- [Cayley graphs](../concepts/cayley-graphs.md) turn generators into edges and group multiplication into graph symmetry.
- [Groups of order 8](../explorations/groups-of-order-eight.md) make the extension problem concrete.
- [Polynomial multiplication as convolution](../explorations/convolution-and-polynomials.md) connects algebra and harmonic analysis.
