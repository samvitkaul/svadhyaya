# Field extensions as linear algebra

An extension $K/F$ makes $K$ a vector space over $F$. If $\alpha$ is algebraic over $F$ with minimal polynomial $m_\alpha(t)$ of degree $d$, then

$$F(\alpha)\cong F[t]/(m_\alpha(t)),\qquad [F(\alpha):F]=d.$$

Multiplication by $\alpha$ is an $F$-linear operator on the $d$-dimensional space $F(\alpha)$; in the power basis its matrix is related to the companion matrix of $m_\alpha$.
