# Modules

A left $R$-module is an abelian group $M$ with a compatible scalar action of a ring $R$. Vector spaces are precisely modules over fields.

The contrast with vector spaces is useful: modules need not admit bases, submodules need not have complements, and short exact sequences need not split.

## Example

The $\mathbb Z$-modules are exactly abelian groups. The module $\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z$ has no $\mathbb Z$-basis; it has torsion.
